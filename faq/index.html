Practical answers to help you compare transport arrangements and prepare your car.

How much does it cost to transport a car in India?

The quote depends on the pickup and delivery locations, car size, carrier availability, transport arrangement and preferred dates. Share these details for a route-specific quote, and confirm pickup, delivery, payment terms and any optional cover before booking.

Can I arrange door-to-door car transport?

Door-to-door pickup and delivery can be coordinated where access and local carrier arrangements allow. If a large carrier cannot reach your address, agree on an accessible handover point and any last-mile movement before booking.

How many days does car transport from Bangalore to Kerala take?

The time depends on your exact Kerala destination, pickup readiness and the carrier schedule. Ask for separate pickup and transit estimates for your dates. Traffic, weather and loading schedules can affect delivery; a route enquiry is needed for a meaningful estimate.

Who manages my car transport?

Hirosh Roadways staff manage your enquiry, pickup coordination, carrier arrangements and delivery updates through our agent and transporter network. Contact our staff for assistance throughout the move; this website does not claim an owned carrier fleet.

Can I transport my SUV from Kochi to Mumbai?

Share your SUV make and model, pickup and delivery PIN codes, and preferred date. Vehicle dimensions and available carrier space determine the suitable arrangement; availability is confirmed with your quote.

Is insurance included in the transport price?

Do not assume insurance is included. Ask what cover, if any, is offered, who provides it, its exclusions, excess and claims process, and request written terms before booking.

What should I do before handing over my car?

Remove personal belongings and valuables, photograph the vehicle, record visible marks and the odometer reading, and keep a signed condition record. Confirm the fuel level, keys and documents required with Hirosh Roadways staff.

Is there a car transport service near me?

Send your pickup city and PIN code, destination and preferred date. HIROSH can check pickup arrangements through its network. A city listed on this website does not mean there is a local HIROSH office.
