Give us your Gurugram address, not just “Delhi NCR”.

Gurugram is in Haryana and forms part of the National Capital Region. For a car moving enquiry, use Gurugram or Gurgaon together with your sector or locality and PIN code. A broad Delhi NCR location leaves the actual pickup or delivery address unclear.

If your address is in Manesar, Sohna, Pataudi or Farrukh Nagar, name that location explicitly. These places appear in the district administration’s area list; listing them here is an invitation to check your address, not confirmation that a carrier can reach every property.

Plan the handover around your building.

Apartment or gated society

Share the sector, society name and vehicle exit requirements. Tell us if the car is in basement parking, whether you can bring it to the gate, and when a handover is possible.

Office or workplace pickup

Confirm who can release the car, the available handover window and any gate pass requirement. Agree on where the vehicle inspection and key handover will take place.

Outlying district address

Include the locality and PIN code rather than only “Gurgaon”. Ask whether local driving to an accessible loading point is needed and whether it is included in your quote.

Car shifting charges: compare the same journey.

A Gurugram pickup and a Delhi pickup can involve different local arrangements even when the destination city is the same. Request a quote for your actual addresses and car model. Include both PIN codes, the car’s running condition, preferred pickup date and any flexibility.

  • Confirm local pickup, carrier movement and destination delivery separately.
  • Ask which carrier type is proposed and whether any part of the journey involves driving your car.
  • Check loading, unloading, taxes and any optional cover or additional charges in the written quote.
  • Record vehicle condition, odometer reading, accessories and keys at handover; agree on the delivery contact.

Use our car transport cost guide and booking safety checklist when comparing car movers and packers. Ask for the vehicle movement to be itemised separately from household goods.

Car relocation in either direction.

For a move out of Gurugram, send the destination city and PIN code. For a move into Gurugram, specify the final sector or locality and the person receiving the car. Our team checks the agent and carrier arrangement for that complete journey; the reverse direction needs its own availability check.

If you need a car container service, discuss the enclosed carrier or dedicated-container requirement before accepting a standard car transport quote.

Gurugram car transport questions

Can I request car pickup in Manesar or Sohna?

Yes, send an enquiry with the exact locality and PIN code. Manesar and Sohna are distinct locations within Gurugram district; a Gurugram city quote should not be assumed to include either. Our team confirms pickup access, local movement and charges for your address.

Do you have a branch office in Gurugram?

Our operations office is in Trivandrum, Kerala. Gurugram enquiries are coordinated through our agent and transporter network. Confirm the pickup contact and agreed handover location with our team before sending the car.

Is a car container service available from Gurgaon?

Ask for a route-specific check. Tell us whether you need enclosed transport or a dedicated container, and ask the team to confirm the actual vehicle type, loading method and availability in writing. A standard carrier quote does not automatically include a container.

How long will car relocation from Gurugram take?

The schedule depends on the destination, pickup arrangement, carrier space and your dates. Request separate estimates for local pickup, carrier departure and destination delivery before booking. No fixed transit time applies to every route.
