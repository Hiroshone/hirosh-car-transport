BEFORE YOU BOOK

What will your car move cost?

Distance is only part of the picture. Your car, the carrier arrangement, pickup access and timing also shape the quote.

Understand costs & charges ↗
How much does it cost to transport a car in India?

The quote depends on the pickup and delivery locations, car size, carrier availability, transport arrangement and preferred dates. Share these details for a route-specific quote, and confirm pickup, delivery, payment terms and any optional cover before booking.

Can I arrange door-to-door car transport?

Door-to-door pickup and delivery can be coordinated where access and local carrier arrangements allow. If a large carrier cannot reach your address, agree on an accessible handover point and any last-mile movement before booking.

How many days does car transport from Bangalore to Kerala take?

The time depends on your exact Kerala destination, pickup readiness and the carrier schedule. Ask for separate pickup and transit estimates for your dates. Traffic, weather and loading schedules can affect delivery; a route enquiry is needed for a meaningful estimate.
