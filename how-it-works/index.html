HIROSH CAR TRANSPORT

A clear plan for your car’s journey.

A good car move starts with the right information and a shared understanding of pickup, transport and delivery.

  1. Tell us your route

    Share both cities, PIN codes, car model and preferred pickup date.

  2. Review the arrangement

    Confirm the quote, carrier option, pickup access and expected schedule before booking.

  3. Prepare & hand over

    Document the car’s condition and confirm the handover details with the transporter.

  4. Receive & inspect

    Coordinate delivery, inspect the car against the handover record and note any concerns.

Before you confirm

  • Provide pickup and delivery cities, PIN codes and access notes.
  • State the car make, model, running condition and preferred date.
  • Review the total quote, inclusions, optional cover and payment terms.
  • Confirm cancellation terms, the expected schedule and your contact for updates.

At pickup and delivery

  • Remove personal items and valuables.
  • Photograph all sides of the car and record existing marks.
  • Record the odometer, fuel level and keys handed over.
  • Agree which documents are needed; do not send sensitive documents through the enquiry form.
  • Inspect the vehicle at delivery and record concerns before completing the handover.

Timing is specific to your route.

Ask for pickup and transit estimates separately. Loading schedules, traffic and weather can affect movement. For a Bangalore-to-Kerala move, include your exact Kerala destination so the schedule reflects the actual journey.
