Car carrier service between cities

Car carrier movement is an option for relocating a car over longer distances without driving the full route yourself. HIROSH coordinates with transporter and car-carrier partners; availability and the movement plan are confirmed for each enquiry.

Before booking, establish whether your quote includes local pickup and delivery, where handover happens, and which parts of the journey involve road driving rather than carrier movement.

Door-to-door car shifting

Share the full pickup and delivery locations, including PIN codes and access restrictions. Narrow roads, gated communities and carrier parking restrictions may require an agreed nearby handover point or a separate local movement arrangement.

Confirm these details and any related charges in writing, so door-to-door means the same thing to everyone involved.

Hatchbacks, sedans and SUVs

The car’s model, size, running condition and ground clearance matter. Mention modifications, non-running condition or special handling needs at the enquiry stage.

For a luxury car or a request for enclosed transport, ask about the available carrier type and handling arrangement. Enclosed availability is route-specific and must be confirmed before booking.

Car carrier transport or driving the car yourself?

A car carrier can reduce the need to drive the full intercity distance yourself. Local pickup or delivery may still involve driving; ask our staff how each part of your move will be handled. If you are comparing options, consider your travel time, vehicle condition, pickup access and the complete transport quote.
