Move your car with your eyes open.

A few checks before payment and handover can help you spot impersonation, misleading quotes and unexpected demands.

01 / VERIFY

A familiar name is not enough

Copied logos, lookalike websites and unofficial phone numbers can impersonate a transport business. Check the domain and use the contact details published on the business’s own website.

02 / COMPARE

Check what a low quote leaves out

Ask about pickup, delivery, carrier movement and optional services. A very low headline figure can hide extra charges. Keep the full quote and agreed payment terms in writing.

03 / CONFIRM

Pause before an unexpected payment

If someone changes the payee, asks for a personal transfer or demands extra money to release your car, contact the company using a previously verified number before paying.

Your pre-booking checklist

  • Verify the business name, website, office address and official contact numbers.
  • Check recent work photos and reviews, while remembering that online reviews alone do not prove identity.
  • Ask for the total quote, inclusions, payment stages, cancellation terms and expected pickup and transit times.
  • Confirm payment instructions directly with staff. Keep the quote, payment acknowledgement and messages.
  • Ask whether any transit cover applies, and read the provider’s terms and exclusions.
  • Confirm the pickup arrangement with company staff before handing over your car.
  • Photograph the vehicle, record the odometer and existing marks, and keep a handover record.

If you suspect fraud

Keep the quote, payment references, messages and handover records. Contact the business through a verified channel. For suspected financial cyber fraud in India, report promptly through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or call 1930.

These are general customer precautions, not allegations against a particular transport provider. Further reading: industry guidance on impersonation and Government of India reporting guidance.
